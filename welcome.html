League hub

Clutch & Ball

Clutch & Ball is a web application for youth basketball leagues and teams. Coaches manage rosters, schedules, and invite codes; parents and players join with a team code and share one hub for RSVPs, game logs, stats, and team updates. Optional Google Calendar sync is available from your account settings after sign-in.

This page is public. You can read about the app without signing in. Sign-in is optional for coaches and families who use the product.

About this application

Clutch & Ball is software for youth basketball leagues and teams: rosters, invite codes, shared schedules, RSVPs, game logs, statistics, in-team communication, and optional Google Calendar sync. Coaches create and manage squads; parents and players join with a code and see the same schedule and updates.
