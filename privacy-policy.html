Privacy Policy

Effective: May 13, 2026 · clutchandball.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Clutch & Ball (“Clutch & Ball,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our websites, apps, and related services (collectively, the “Service”). By using the Service, you acknowledge this Policy.

Who we are

Clutch & Ball is a youth basketball team and community platform for leagues, teams, schedules, rosters, stats, communication, and related features. The Service is offered at clutchandball.com and associated properties we operate.

Information we collect

We collect information in three broad categories: what you give us, what is generated when you use the Service, and what we receive from integrations and infrastructure.

  • Account and profile data. When you register or sign in, we collect identifiers and profile details needed to operate accounts—such as email address, display name, authentication data, and preferences you set.
  • Team, league, and activity data. Information you or your organization add to the Service, including rosters, schedules, game logs, statistics, RSVPs, messages or posts where available, files or media you upload, and similar content tied to teams or events.
  • Support and communications. Messages you send us (for example email to support or privacy contacts) and metadata needed to respond.
  • Technical and security data. Server and application logs, device or browser type, IP address, timestamps, and diagnostic data from hosting and security tools. We use this to run the Service, troubleshoot, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

Why we use information

We use the categories above to:

  • Create and manage accounts, authenticate users, and deliver core product features.
  • Host, sync, and display team and league content to the people you choose to share it with.
  • Send service-related notices (security alerts, policy updates, transactional messages) and optional notifications you or your organization enable (such as schedule or RSVP reminders).
  • Maintain security, enforce our Terms of Service, detect fraud or misuse, and comply with law.
  • Analyze aggregated or de-identified usage to improve performance and product experience.

Google account and Google Calendar

If you choose to link a Google account or connect Google Calendar, Google’s OAuth consent screen will list the specific permissions you approve. We only use Google access for the purposes described at authorization time and in this Policy.

For Calendar, we use Google APIs to create, update, or remove calendar events on your connected Google calendar when you take corresponding actions in Clutch & Ball (for example saving or editing a team event). We store OAuth tokens and related credentials securely so the Service can perform those operations on your behalf until you disconnect.

We do not use Google Calendar access to read your email, browse Gmail, access Google Drive files unrelated to the Service, or collect advertising profiles from Google. We do not sell Google account data.

You may disconnect Google Calendar from your Account settings at any time. Disconnecting stops new sync from our systems; events already written to Google Calendar remain there until you delete them in Google.

Google’s own terms and privacy notices apply to your use of Google services. See Google’s documentation for details on OAuth data and API user data policies.

Where and how data is stored

The Service uses Supabase for database storage, authentication, and related backend infrastructure. We may also use other subprocessors (such as email delivery or analytics) strictly to operate the Service. Data may be processed in the United States and in other regions where those providers maintain facilities, consistent with their documentation and our agreements with them.

Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • Service providers. With vendors who process data on our instructions to host, secure, or support the Service (including Supabase and, when you enable it, Google).
  • Within the product. With coaches, parents, players, or league administrators according to how teams and permissions are configured—so the platform works as a shared sports tool.
  • Legal and safety. When we believe disclosure is required by law, legal process, or government request, or is necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of users, Clutch & Ball, or the public.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality and continuity commitments where feasible.

Minors and schools

Clutch & Ball is built for youth basketball communities. Many users are minors or share information about minors (for example roster entries).

  • Parents, guardians, leagues, and schools should ensure account setup and sharing of personal information comply with applicable laws and internal policies regarding children and students.
  • Where consent or authorization is required for a minor’s information, the adult creating or approving the account should provide it.
  • If you believe information about a minor was submitted without appropriate authority, contact privacy@clutchandball.com with enough detail for us to investigate. We will take appropriate steps consistent with law and the situation.

Security

We implement administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information, including encryption in transit for web traffic, access controls on production systems, and least-privilege practices for staff and infrastructure. No online service can guarantee absolute security; if we learn of a breach that affects you where notification is required, we will follow applicable law.

Retention and account deletion

We retain information for as long as your account is active, as needed to provide the Service, and as required by law or legitimate business needs (such as security logs or financial records).

Where the product supports it, you may delete your account. Deletion removes or anonymizes personal data associated with your account in active systems within a reasonable period, subject to limited retention for backups, fraud prevention, or legal obligations. Some content you contributed to shared team spaces may remain visible to others in de-identified or aggregate form where removal is technically impractical; team administrators may also retain copies outside our Service.

Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction. Update profile and team information in the app where editing is available, or ask us for a copy of personal data we hold about you.
  • Deletion. Request account deletion through in-product controls where offered, or by emailing privacy@clutchandball.com.
  • Integrations. Revoke Google access through your Account page and through your Google account security settings.
  • Marketing. We send only product-related or transactional messages unless you opt in to broader marketing where we offer it; you can opt out of non-essential email using unsubscribe links when provided.
  • Regional rights. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (for example to object to certain processing or to lodge a complaint with a regulator). Contact us and we will respond in line with applicable law.

International users

If you access the Service from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries where we or our vendors operate. We take steps designed to ensure appropriate safeguards where required.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect product, legal, or operational changes. We will revise the “Effective” date above and, when changes are material, provide additional notice (such as an in-app message or email) where practical. Continued use of the Service after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy unless law requires express consent.
